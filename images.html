image formats

by Oscar Martinez Vandezande

Animated Portable Network Graphics

Also known as a .apng, they are a choice for better animation sequences. Such as smoother animation, and overall better preformance. However, .apngs are not typically supported on most browsers, having less support.

AV1 Image File Format

AV1, is also known as .avif, AV1 is a good choicefor both still and animated images as it’s a free format and has high preformace results. it has better compression than both Pngs and Jpegs. It allows for frames, colors, and transparency.

Graphics Interchange Format

Also known as .gif, GIF’s are a nice choice when it comes to very simplistic and short animations. they’re typically lower in pixel quality but are commonly used.

Joint Photographic Expert Group Image

More commonly known as .jpg currently is the most commonly used and popular format of imaging. However, the photos occasionally do have lowered quality but they take up little space.

Portable Network Graphics

Known as .png, png’s are also very common and typically preffered over .jpegs and have the ability to have transparency in areas, and better compression and reproduction.

Scalable Vector Graphics

.svg, a image formating focused on vector images using elements, icons, diagrams, that must be drawn accurately.

Web Picture Format

.webp a great choice for images both still ancd animated. .Webp allows for much better compression, with support for higher color, frames, depth, and transparency. .AVIF may offer better compression but isnt well supported and doesnt have progressive rendering, making .Wepb a little better, but also not having as much support on web browsers.
